What AQL level do we use for final inspection of knitwear orders, and what counts as a major defect?
Final inspection of knitwear uses AQL 2.5 for major defects and 4.0 for minor defects, at General Inspection Level II.1 Major defects are those likely to cause a return or complaint, including:
  • Holes, open seams or broken stitches in visible areas2
  • Shade variation visible at arm’s length between panels2
  • Wrong size, care or country-of-origin labels1
